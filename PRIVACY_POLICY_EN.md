# Money Jotter Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

## Introduction

Money Jotter ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information Collection

### Information We Collect

**The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.**

- **Game Data**: All game records, player information, scores, and other data are stored locally on your device (using SQLite database) and are not uploaded to any server.
- **No Account System**: The App does not require registration or login, so we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or other personal information.

### Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect certain information:

#### Google AdMob (Advertising Service)

- **Purpose**: Display advertisements to support the free operation of the App
- **Information Collected**:
  - Device information (device model, operating system version)
  - Advertising identifier (Advertising ID / IDFA)
  - IP address
  - Ad interaction information
- **Privacy Policy**: [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Opt-Out**:
  - **iOS**: Settings > Privacy > Tracking > Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  - **Android**: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads

#### Google Play Services (Android Only)

- **Purpose**: Provide core app functionality
- **Information Collected**: According to Google Play Services policy
- **Privacy Policy**: [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

#### Firebase Analytics (Usage Analytics)

- **Purpose**: Understand how users interact with the App to improve user experience
- **Information Collected**:
  - App usage events (e.g., starting a game, completing rounds, ending games)
  - Device information (device model, operating system version)
  - App version information
  - Approximate location (based on IP address, country/region level only)
- **Information NOT Collected**:
  - Player names or game content
  - Personally identifiable information
- **Privacy Policy**: [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

#### Firebase Crashlytics (Crash Reporting)

- **Purpose**: Collect crash reports to help us fix issues and improve app stability
- **Information Collected**:
  - Crash logs and stack traces
  - Device information (device model, operating system version, memory state)
  - App version information
- **Information NOT Collected**:
  - Game data or player information
  - Personally identifiable information
- **Privacy Policy**: [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## Information Use

### Local Data Use

- Game records and player information are used solely for in-app features such as displaying scores and history
- This data is completely stored on your device and you can clear all data at any time by deleting the App

### Advertising

- We use Google AdMob to display advertisements
- Ads may be personalized based on your device information and usage
- You can opt out of personalized ads (see "Opt-Out" instructions above)

## Information Sharing

**We do not actively share, sell, or rent your personal information.**

The only possible information sharing is through third-party services (such as Google AdMob), which have their own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## Data Security

- All game data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's operating system
- We do not have servers, so there is no risk of data breaches
- We recommend backing up your device regularly to prevent data loss

## Your Rights

You have the following rights:

- **Access**: You can view your game data within the App at any time
- **Deletion**: You can completely clear all data by deleting the App
- **Opt-Out**: You can opt out of personalized ads (see instructions above)
